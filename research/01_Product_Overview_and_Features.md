# ClickSupply: Product Overview & Core Features

## 1. Product Vision
**ClickSupply** is an enterprise-grade Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform tailored for the Indian digital market. It shifts brands from traditional SEO (ranking for clicks) to AEO (being cited as the authoritative answer in zero-click environments). 

## 2. Core Value Proposition
With AI referral traffic converting at up to 4.4x the rate of traditional organic traffic, ClickSupply enables Indian brands, PR teams, and SEO agencies to monitor, analyze, and optimize their visibility across global and indigenous AI search engines.

## 3. Core Modules & Dashboards

### 3.1. Share of Model (SoM) Tracker
*   **Concept:** The AI-era equivalent of "Share of Voice". It calculates `(Brand Citations / Total Citations) * 100`.
*   **Generative Position:** Tracks the numerical rank of a brand when an AI outputs a listicle or comparison (e.g., being #1 vs #5).
*   **Sentiment Analysis:** Analyzes whether the AI frames the brand positively, neutrally, or negatively.

### 3.2. Conversation Explorer & Prompt Volumes
*   **Prompt Volumes:** Replaces traditional search volume by revealing what conversational, long-tail questions (7-70 words) users are asking AI interfaces.
*   **Query Fan-Out Mapping:** Tracks how a base query (e.g., "best CRM") is expanded into multiple sub-queries by engines like Google AI Mode and Perplexity.

### 3.3. Agent Analytics (Server-Side Logs)
*   **Bot Tracking:** Ingests server logs (AWS/Cloudflare) to track exactly how and when AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot scrape the client's site.
*   **Technical GEO Audit:** Flags complex JavaScript (DOM rendering issues) that block fast passage extraction by AI agents. 

### 3.4. RAG Diagnostics & Simulation Lab
*   **Multi-Model Simulation:** Allows users to run synthetic prompts across multiple LLMs simultaneously to see how different models retrieve and summarize their data.
*   **Citation Audit:** Identifies the exact third-party URLs (e.g., Reddit, G2, Trustpilot) the AI uses to source information about the brand.
